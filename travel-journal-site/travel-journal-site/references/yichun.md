# Yichun — Forest

## Character

Quiet forest roads, cool mist, dense tree cover, and a slow restorative rhythm.

## Direction

- Palette: pine green, moss, fog grey, warm wood, restrained cream.
- Motifs: forest canopy, contour lines, mist, bark texture, winding roads.
- Photography: deep natural layers, filtered light, road-scale details, generous negative space.
- Typography: calm, spacious, literary.
- Motion: slow parallax, soft fades, restrained pointer disturbance.
- Map: subdued roads on deep green terrain; fog-like gradients may soften its boundary with the journal.

Keep motion organic rather than springy or mechanical.

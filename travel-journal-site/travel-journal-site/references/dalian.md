# Dalian — Coast

## Character

Open coastline, sea wind, changing light, harbor infrastructure, and a brighter travel rhythm.

## Direction

- Palette: sea blue, sand, white, slate, restrained sunset coral.
- Motifs: coastline curves, sea haze, harbor lights, coastal roads.
- Photography: open horizons, reflective water, geometric harbor details, evening light.
- Typography: clean, airy, slightly energetic.
- Motion: gentle horizontal drift and tide-like transitions; avoid cartoon-style waves.
- Map: pale coastal terrain with an emphasized shoreline and restrained sea-color overlays.
